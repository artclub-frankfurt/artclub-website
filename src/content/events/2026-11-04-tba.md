We’re excited to invite you to our next Art Club event! This time, we’re visiting *Atelierfrankfurt* in Ostend.

With over 220 artists and creatives, it is the largest art center in Hesse. In a former warehouse, around 150 studios are spread out under one roof.

We’ll get a tour of the building and the current exhibitions, led by Director Corinna Bimboese herself. Among other things, we’ll learn how the building works and how people work there.

You can find a first overview of the current exhibitions here: [https://www.atelierfrankfurt.de/programm/](https://www.atelierfrankfurt.de/programm/)

We’re looking forward to discovering the Atelierfrankfurt together with you!
